# Lotus Website

Create a modern, premium SaaS website in Portuguese (pt-BR) for an accounting and business advisory company. The overall experience should communicate trust, intelligence, professionalism, innovation, and clarity. The design should feel comparable to leading technology companies rather than traditional accounting firms, combining elegant minimalism with refined visual details and subtle premium interactions.

The website should include the following sections in this order:

1. Hero Section

Create a visually striking hero section with a full-width, high-quality photograph of professionals collaborating inside a modern office environment. The image should feel bright, sophisticated, and authentic, avoiding generic stock-photo aesthetics.

Display the company's main slogan prominently:

"Transformando números em decisões"

Use a large, elegant headline and a supporting paragraph explaining that the company helps businesses transform accounting, financial information, and strategic planning into confident business decisions.

Include two prominent CTA buttons using modern glassmorphism effects with soft blur, subtle transparency, smooth hover animations, and refined shadows.

Suggested CTAs:

Get a Free Consultation

Explore Our Services

The navigation bar should be transparent ("floating") so that only the company logo, navigation links, and the "Entrar" button are visible over the hero image.

The "Entrar" button must redirect to:

https://lotusaccounting.lovable.app/

The navigation menu should include:

Home

About

Services

Pricing

Testimonials

News

Contact

A Light/Dark Mode toggle should also appear in the navigation bar.

2. About Us

Create an elegant "About Us" section introducing the company.

Suggested heading:

Accounting Designed for Modern Businesses

Suggested content:

"Our mission is to transform financial information into strategic decisions. By combining accounting expertise, technology, and business intelligence, we help entrepreneurs gain clarity, reduce uncertainty, and focus on sustainable growth."

Use a modern two-column layout with rounded image frames showing business meetings, financial analysis, or consulting professionals.

3. Services

Present services as modern premium cards with rounded corners, subtle shadows, soft gradients, and elegant hover animations.

Include services such as:

Business Accounting

Modern icon

Short title

Brief professional description

Learn More button

Why Companies Trust Us

Create three premium pricing cards.

Visual Design

The website should feel modern, luxurious, clean, and technology-oriented.

Primary color palette:

Light Theme

Background:
#f1f1f1

Dark Theme

Background:
#000000

Accent colors:

#070f32
#516989
#5d503a

Use these colors consistently throughout buttons, highlights, decorative elements, borders, and subtle gradients.

The Light/Dark Mode toggle must smoothly transition between themes.

When the theme changes:

switch the complete color palette

switch text colors

switch backgrounds

switch icons when appropriate

automatically swap the company logo using the attached light and dark logo files

Typography

Use:

Titles:

Open Sauce

Codec Pro

Body text:

Inter

Typography should feel spacious, premium, and highly readable.

Use generous white space, balanced layouts, and clear content hierarchy.

Images

Use only premium-quality office and business imagery.

Images should feature:

modern offices

executives collaborating

financial consulting

business meetings

professionals analyzing reports

technology-driven workspaces

Avoid clichéd stock photos.

All images should use rounded corners with elegant framing.

UI Components

The website should include:

Smooth scrolling

Subtle parallax where appropriate

Glassmorphism CTA buttons

Rounded cards

Soft shadows

Premium hover effects

Elegant transitions

Responsive layout

Maintain a refined visual rhythm with consistent spacing, modern component sizing, and polished interactions throughout the experience.

Tone of the Copy

All written content should be concise, confident, and professional.

Avoid marketing clichés or exaggerated claims.

The messaging should communicate credibility, expertise, clarity, and strategic partnership, making visitors feel they are engaging with a modern accounting firm that combines financial excellence with technology and business intelligence.
